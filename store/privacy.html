drafted. Capture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026 · Drafted Inc.

drafted. Capture ("the extension") is a browser-interaction recorder that helps people produce computer-use training data from their own browsing. This policy explains what it handles and where that data goes.

What the extension records

Only while you have capture turned on, the extension records your interactions in the browser: mouse clicks and their coordinates, keystrokes, scrolling, page navigation, the tab and URL each action occurred on, and screenshots of the visible page. Capture is fully under your control and is off by default.

Where your data goes

Your recordings stay on your device. Sessions and settings are stored locally using the browser's extension storage and IndexedDB. The extension does not send your recordings to Drafted's servers or any third party. Data leaves your machine only when you explicitly choose to export it as a file you save yourself, or, if you configure an optional API key, when you choose to upload to a destination you control. Optional AI lab API keys you enter are sent only to that lab's own endpoint and never to Drafted.

Privacy controls

  • Password fields are masked in the recorded data.
  • A domain blocklist lets you exclude any sites from capture entirely.
  • Capture never runs unless you turn it on, and you can stop at any time.
  • You can delete any recorded session from the review panel.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending.
  • We do not use remote code; all extension code runs locally from the package.
